K-Means kya hai?

Unsupervised learning ka algorithm hai — bina labels ke data ko groups (clusters) me baanta hai.

  1. K centroids (markaz) randomly chuno.
  2. Har point ko sab se qareebi centroid se joro.
  3. Har cluster ka naya markaz (average) nikalo.
  4. Centroids ruk jayen to stop — clusters ready!
